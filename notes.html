Behind the Build

Why TerritoryPulse was proposed, who it is for, the workflow it aims to improve, the requirements guiding the demo, and how it compares with existing alternatives.

The problem and who has it

1Target user and potential buyer

Target user: Territory representatives working in the Irish private healthcare sector who manage referrals from GP practices and individual consultants. They review referral activity, maintain professional relationships and decide which sources to contact or visit next.

Potential buyer: A private healthcare organisation, or its territory manager or business development manager, who is responsible for referral growth and territory performance. The organisation would potentially pay for the tool, while the territory representative would use it day to day.

2The problem in the user's words

Based on my experience working in private healthcare, I can receive more than 100 referrals per week. I manually review referral PDFs and record details in a spreadsheet. The biggest challenge is not just recording referrals, but deciding which GP practices or individual consultants I should contact next and where to focus my time.
Evidence note: This is based on my own description of my work. If presenting it as a direct quotation, ensure the wording is genuinely mine. It is not feedback from other users.

3Current process and difficulties

The current process involves reviewing referral PDFs, manually recording referral details in a spreadsheet and reviewing referral performance with the territory manager.

  • Manual data entry: Reviewing and recording a high volume of referrals takes time.
  • Limited visibility: Referral activity across multiple GP practices and individual consultants can be difficult to compare.
  • Identifying changes: Recognising declining referral activity may require manually comparing spreadsheet records.
  • Contact prioritisation: Deciding which sources need attention next can be difficult without a consistent overview.
  • Follow-up tracking: Keeping track of contact dates and planned actions can make weekly territory planning more challenging.
These are observations about the process described, not measured findings about time lost or error rates.

Area 2: The solution and how it fits

TerritoryPulse is a lightweight referral-source tracker and territory-planning tool. It is designed to help private healthcare territory representatives turn referral activity and contact history into a clearer weekly action list. It is a planning aid, not a clinical system or a replacement for a referral-management platform.

1Record referral activity

The representative records a referral against the relevant GP practice or individual consultant. The prototype uses fictional example records only.

2Review activity and follow-ups

TerritoryPulse calculates referral totals, compares the latest 30 days with the previous 30 days, shows contact recency and highlights outstanding or overdue follow-up actions.

3Plan the next contact

The representative reviews the suggested priorities and their explanations, plans a call, visit or email, and can review contact history. The priority rules are illustrative and have not been validated with users.

How the solution fits the real working situation

Territory representatives may need to check information while travelling between visits or during a busy working day. The interface uses responsive layouts for phone and desktop screens, clear navigation, concise summaries and a direct route to the interactive demo. Tables can scroll horizontally on smaller screens rather than forcing the whole page to widen. The prototype should be tested on a phone by an intended user to confirm that the workflow is genuinely convenient.

Data and limits: All sample records are fictional. Do not enter patient-identifiable information or real referral PDFs. The demo is a prototype; its scoring rules are illustrative, and its usefulness and time-saving potential have not yet been measured.

How TerritoryPulse could be sustained

Proposed model: a business-to-business subscription paid by a private healthcare organisation for its territory representatives.

Illustrative pricing assumption: €49 per representative per month. At that assumed rate, ten representatives would cost €490 per month (€5,880 per year), before costs and tax. This is a hypothesis for discussion, not a validated price or revenue forecast.

The organisation might pay if the tool helps representatives review referral trends and follow-ups with less manual effort. Time saved and willingness to pay have not been measured; these assumptions should be tested with potential users and buyers.

5Existing products and alternatives

Practice Referral Website checked: 26 October 2026

Practice Referral — Referral Intelligence ↗

Practice Referral provides referral intelligence for specialist practices. Its website describes tools for understanding referral networks, identifying potential GP referrers, analysing competitor referral relationships and targeting outreach campaigns.

Comparison with TerritoryPulse: Both aim to turn referral information into actionable outreach. Practice Referral focuses more on referral-network intelligence and identifying new opportunities, while TerritoryPulse focuses on a representative's day-to-day workflow: recording referral activity, reviewing trends, tracking contacts and planning follow-ups.

Doctivity Health Website checked: 26 October 2026

Doctivity Health ↗

Doctivity Health provides physician relationship management and referral intelligence for healthcare organisations. Its platform describes provider outreach tracking, referral analytics, relationship management and performance reporting.

Comparison with TerritoryPulse: Both support referral-source prioritisation and relationship management. Doctivity offers a broader organisational platform with market intelligence and reporting capabilities, whereas TerritoryPulse is a lightweight prototype focused on an individual representative's referral tracking and weekly planning workflow.

What this comparison tells us: TerritoryPulse is not the first product to address referral intelligence or provider relationship management. Its proposed distinction is a simpler, focused workflow for representatives who want to turn referral records into an explainable weekly action list. This potential advantage still needs to be tested with intended users. Recheck competitor websites before submission in case their features have changed.
