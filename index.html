The challenge

Referral activity is only useful when it leads to a next step.

When referral records and contact notes live in separate places, it can take extra effort to see which sources have changed, who has not been contacted recently, and which follow-ups are due.

↗

Compare recent referral counts with the previous period to spot sources that may need a closer look.

✓

How it works

A straightforward workflow for territory planning.

TerritoryPulse is designed as a lightweight planning aid—not a replacement for a clinical or referral-management system.

STEP 01

Record referral activity

Associate fictional referral records with a GP practice, individual consultant or other relevant source.

STEP 02

Review the signals

See activity comparisons, time since last contact and overdue follow-up actions in one place.

STEP 03

Plan the next contact

Use the review list and visit planner to organise what to follow up, then record the outcome.

Sustainability

A simple business model to test.

TerritoryPulse could be sustained as a business-to-business subscription for private healthcare organisations that want a lightweight territory-planning workflow.

Illustrative pricing assumption

€49 per representative per month

This is a hypothetical starting price for discussion, not a researched market price or an existing offer. An organisation could pay for its representatives to use the tool.

For example, 10 seats at this assumed price would produce €490 per month before costs. This is revenue arithmetic only—not a forecast or profit estimate.

Why an organisation might pay

The proposed value is less manual effort bringing referral activity and follow-up plans together, plus a clearer weekly view of which sources may need attention.

Time saved and willingness to pay have not yet been measured. These assumptions should be tested with intended users and potential buyers before treating the model as viable.

Designed with care

Practical, transparent and privacy-conscious.

Clear, explainable rules

Priority indicators use simple illustrative rules based on referral changes, contact recency and overdue actions. These thresholds are examples and have not been clinically or commercially validated.

Demo data only

This prototype uses fictional sample records. Do not enter patient-identifiable information or confidential business information. It is not connected to a clinical system and does not provide clinical advice.
